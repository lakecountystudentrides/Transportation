Lake County Student Rides is seeking partnerships with local businesses, organizations, and community leaders who want to help support student transportation in our community.

  • Sponsor fuel costs for community routes
  • Sponsor safety equipment and supplies

Benefits for Sponsors

  • Social media acknowledgment
  • Community impact visibility
  • Support for local families and students
