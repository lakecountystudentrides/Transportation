# Safety System

Safety is the actual product here — the transportation is the delivery mechanism. Every feature below is labeled by whether Florida law requires it, or whether it's a trust/quality standard you're choosing to hold yourself to (see `01-legal-compliance.md` for full citations).

## Driver qualification (before first trip)
- Valid FL driver's license appropriate to vehicle — **required**
- Clean MVR pulled at hire + annually — **recommended / insurer-required**
- Level 2-equivalent background check (state + national criminal history, sex-offender registry) via a licensed live-scan vendor — **recommended for independent operation; legally required if/when contracting with a school district or licensed facility**
- Pre-hire + random drug/alcohol screening — **recommended**
- CPR + Pediatric First Aid certification, renewed every 2 years — **recommended (required for daycare-facility drivers under Ch. 65C-22; hold yourself to the same bar)**
- Signed driver code of conduct — **your own policy**

## Vehicle standards
- Commercial auto insurance, for-hire/livery rated — **required** the moment you accept payment
- Age/size-appropriate child restraints (car seat/booster) installed and inspected — **required by F.S. 316.613/316.614**
- Documented pre-trip inspection (tires, brakes, seat belts/anchors, child door locks, first-aid kit, fire extinguisher, working AC — non-negotiable in Florida) — **your own policy, industry best practice**
- No school-bus markings/equipment (you are not a school bus — see legal doc) — **do not add**

## Parent authorization & verification
- Parent Service Agreement signed before first trip — **your own policy, your primary legal protection**
- Per-child authorization form: authorized pickup/drop-off adults, standing instructions, transportation-safety-relevant medical info only — **your own policy**
- Pickup PIN + authorized-adult manual verification at drop-off — **your own policy** (see Website doc for method comparison)
- Hard rule: never release a child to anyone not on the authorized list, regardless of who calls or claims urgency — **non-negotiable driver training rule**

## Trip operations
- Child check-in confirmation at pickup, check-out confirmation at drop-off, both timestamped and GPS-logged — **your own system**
- Live trip status shared with the enrolled parent only, during active trips only (see Website doc for privacy-conscious tracking design) — **your own system, with written parental consent**
- No unauthorized passengers, no unscheduled stops, no personal errands with a child aboard — **driver code of conduct**
- No phone use while children are in the vehicle (hands-free navigation only, set before departure) — **driver code of conduct**
- No smoking/vaping in vehicle, ever — **driver code of conduct + practical necessity for insurance/AC/cleanliness**

## Emergency preparedness
- Written emergency procedure covering: accident/injury, child not present at expected pickup, unauthorized person attempting pickup, medical event en route, severe weather (relevant in Central FL — have a lightning/storm delay policy), vehicle breakdown
- Emergency contacts (parent + secondary) accessible to the driver for every active trip via the dashboard
- Basic first-aid kit + emergency contact card in vehicle at all times
- Incident report form (what happened, when, who was involved, what action was taken) completed and filed within 24 hours of any incident, however minor, and shared with the affected parent

## Incident reporting & continuous improvement
- Every incident, near-miss, or parent complaint logged in a simple tracker (even a spreadsheet at launch) — review monthly, feed into driver training updates
- Annual (minimum) re-check of MVR, and re-verification that insurance/background-check credentials haven't lapsed

## What to tell parents (this is your marketing copy, drawn straight from this list)
"Every GuardianRide Lake driver passes a background check, holds current CPR/First Aid certification, and drives a commercially insured vehicle equipped with the right car seat or booster for your child. Every trip is logged from pickup to drop-off, and we never release your child to anyone you haven't authorized." — that sentence, essentially verbatim, should appear on your homepage.
