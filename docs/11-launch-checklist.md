# Final Recommendations & 30-Day Launch Checklist

## MY RECOMMENDED BUSINESS MODEL
Direct-to-parent, online booking + recurring weekly/monthly scheduling, single owner-operator vehicle at launch, expanding via employed (not misclassified-contractor) drivers as demand proves out. Position on safety credentials first, convenience second, price third.

## MY RECOMMENDED VEHICLE
The van you already own, provided it seats 15 or fewer including the driver (keeps you outside CDL/school-bus-adjacent territory). If you're choosing between vehicles for a second unit later, a minivan or mid-size passenger van in the 6–8 child capacity range fits this business's actual route sizes better than a large 12–15 seat van.

## MY RECOMMENDED STARTUP BUDGET
**Moderate tier** (~$1,800–$3,700 launch cash + ~$450–$830/month recurring) — see `08-startup-budget.md`.

## MY RECOMMENDED PRICING
Base $6 + $2.25/mile + $0.35/min, round trip at 1.85x, 10–15% recurring discounts, minimum fare $12. Published headline: **$700–$850/month per child for full round-trip school-day recurring service.** See `03-pricing.md`.

## MY RECOMMENDED WEBSITE SYSTEM
WordPress or Wix for the marketing site; a no-code app platform (Bubble/Softr) wired to Stripe for booking, payment, and driver/parent dashboards at launch; migrate to custom software once revenue justifies it. See `05-website-technology.md`.

## MY REQUIRED LICENSES/PERMITS
Florida LLC (done) + Lake County Local Business Tax Receipt + city BTR if applicable + **confirmed answer from Lake County on whether a vehicle-for-hire permit applies to you** (unresolved, call before launch — see `01-legal-compliance.md`).

## MY REQUIRED INSURANCE
Commercial auto (for-hire/livery-rated, $1M+ recommended), general liability ($1M/$2M) with **Sexual Abuse & Molestation coverage named explicitly**, workers' comp once you hire employees.

## MY REQUIRED DRIVER QUALIFICATIONS
Valid FL license, clean MVR, background check (Level 2-equivalent), CPR/First Aid certification, signed code of conduct — treat all as required even where not strictly state-mandated for an independent operator.

## MY SAFETY SYSTEM
Background-checked/insured/car-seat-equipped vehicle, parent authorization + authorized-pickup verification, PIN-based handoff confirmation, GPS+timestamp trip logging with parental consent, incident reporting process. See `06-safety-system.md`.

## MY FIRST 25 CUSTOMERS PLAN
Personal network outreach + local Facebook parent groups + Google Business Profile + in-person daycare/school front-office visits + founding-family discount. See `09-marketing-plan.md`.

## MY FIRST 30 DAYS
Legal/insurance confirmation → website/booking build → safety equipment/credentials → soft-launch marketing → first paid bookings. Full day-by-day below.

## Day-by-day 30-day launch checklist

**Days 1–3 — Legal & insurance groundwork**
1. Call Lake County Office of Business Licensing: ask the vehicle-for-hire permit question directly (see `01-legal-compliance.md`). Email a follow-up to get the answer in writing.
2. Call/email your city's clerk office if your business address is inside a municipality — ask about a municipal BTR.
3. Apply for the Lake County Local Business Tax Receipt.

**Days 4–6 — Insurance**
4. Get 3 commercial auto insurance quotes from agents with NEMT/child-transport experience; explicitly ask about Sexual Abuse & Molestation coverage on each.
5. Get general liability quotes (often bundled with the same agent).
6. Select and bind commercial auto + GL coverage before driving a single paid trip.

**Days 7–9 — Driver credentials (you, as the initial driver)**
7. Book a Level 2-equivalent background check through a local FDLE-authorized live-scan vendor.
8. Book/complete a CPR + Pediatric First Aid certification course.
9. Schedule an MVR pull through your insurer or a background-check vendor.

**Days 10–12 — Vehicle & child safety equipment**
10. Deep-clean and safety-inspect the van (tires, brakes, seat belts, AC, first-aid kit, fire extinguisher).
11. Purchase and professionally install appropriate car seats/boosters (cover the age range you expect to serve first).
12. Set up a business phone number (Google Voice or dedicated line) and a basic in-vehicle emergency contact card.

**Days 13–16 — Legal documents**
13. Draft (or have an attorney draft/review) the Parent Service Agreement and per-child authorization form.
14. Draft a Privacy Policy and Terms of Service covering data collection, GPS/tracking consent, and cancellation policy.
15. Spend the ~30–60 minute attorney consult confirming the AG opinion's application to your model and reviewing your contracts.
16. Finalize your business name after a Sunbiz + trademark + social-handle availability check (see `02-branding-names.md`).

**Days 17–21 — Website & booking system build**
17. Set up the marketing website (WordPress/Wix) with the homepage structure from `04-website-booking-system.md`.
18. Build the booking flow on your chosen no-code platform (8 steps, pricing formula, Stripe integration).
19. Build the driver dashboard (Today's Trips, Start/Arrived/Complete Trip actions).
20. Build the parent dashboard (upcoming rides, saved children/addresses, payment history, recurring schedule management).
21. Test the full flow yourself end-to-end at least 3 times, including a recurring-schedule setup and a round-trip booking, before any real customer touches it.

**Days 22–24 — Safety system finalization**
22. Write and internalize your driver code of conduct and emergency procedures (even as a solo driver — you need this documented for insurance and for when you hire driver #2).
23. Set up Google Business Profile fully (photos, service area, hours, category).
24. Set up Facebook/Instagram business pages with your safety-credential messaging drafted and ready to post.

**Days 25–27 — Soft launch outreach**
25. Personally message your existing network (friends/neighbors/community contacts) about the launch and founding-family offer.
26. Post genuine introduction posts in 3–5 local Facebook parent groups.
27. Visit 5–10 local daycares/after-school programs in person to introduce the service and leave materials, following each front office's posted vendor policy.

**Days 28–30 — First bookings**
28. Confirm your first 1–3 real bookings through the actual website flow (not a manual workaround) — this is your real system test.
29. Run the first paid trip end-to-end: booking → payment confirmed → Start Trip → parent notification → Complete Trip → receipt. Fix anything that broke.
30. Ask your first completed customer(s) for a Google review and, if comfortable, a testimonial for your website/social pages — and start the referral-program conversation with them for customer #2 onward.
