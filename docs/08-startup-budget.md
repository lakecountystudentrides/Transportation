## Recommendation
Start at **Moderate**, not Low-Cost or Professional. Low-Cost under-invests in the two things that actually determine whether parents trust you (insurance quality/SAM coverage, and a website that doesn't look like a hobby project); Professional spends money on polish (vehicle wraps, custom software) before you've validated demand. Moderate gets you a real commercial insurance policy, a credible booking website, background/CPR credentials, and enough marketing spend to run the first-25-customers plan (see `09-marketing-plan.md`) — upgrade toward Professional-tier line items (telematics, custom app, second driver) once recurring revenue is actually funding it.
