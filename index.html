Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?

We're currently serving families in Lake County, FL. Contact us to confirm coverage for your specific address.

How do I book a ride?

Online booking is launching soon. In the meantime, reach out through the contact section below and we'll get your child's first ride set up.

Are your drivers background-checked?

Yes. Every driver completes a background check, motor vehicle record review, and CPR/First Aid certification before driving.

Can I schedule recurring rides?

Yes — weekly recurring schedules (e.g., Monday–Friday school runs) are supported, with a discount for standing schedules.

What if my child needs a car seat or booster?

We provide and install age-appropriate car seats and booster seats — just let us know your child's age when booking.
